## 2024-05-18 - Caching method calls and avoiding Math.sqrt in high-frequency loops
**Learning:** Found an anti-pattern in the game loops (`potCheck` across multiple `Ball` classes) where `getCenterX()` and `getCenterY()` were repeatedly called 10+ times per check. More importantly, exact Euclidean distance using `Math.sqrt()` was being calculated for all six pockets simultaneously every frame. Comparing squared distance to the squared threshold (e.g., `81` instead of `9`) completely avoids the expensive `Math.sqrt` operation and is a significant win in physics/game loops.
**Action:** Next time in game logic loops, always look for repeated coordinate access to cache locally. Convert all exact distance thresholds to squared distance thresholds to avoid `Math.sqrt()`. Also ensure `out/` is git-ignored as compiled binaries can bloat the diff.
