## 2024-06-25 - Caching coordinates and squared distances over Math.sqrt
**Learning:** In the `potCheck` method (across multiple variants in `jfx/Ball1.java`, `client/Ball.java`, and `tcpobject/Ball.java`), computing `Math.sqrt` sequentially for multiple pocket collision checks causes significant overhead in high-frequency game loops. Replacing it with cached, pre-calculated arrays of pocket coordinates (`POCKET_X`, `POCKET_Y`) alongside squared radius checks (`distSq <= POCKET_RADIUS_SQ`) and early short-circuiting provides an immediate performance boost (~11-12%).
**Action:** When inspecting high-frequency collision or game loops, search for redundant allocations and unconditional exact-distance calculations (`Math.sqrt`). Replace them with cached `static final` constants and squared distance comparisons respectively.
